The things people ask before they walk in

Answered the way we'd answer them at the door. You won't be over sold.

Who is it for?

In short, everyone.

  • 60+ wanting to find that fitter, stronger you again
  • A 30 to 45 year old tired parent
  • Overweight and feeling really tired and low
  • Never trained before and want to learn properly
  • Training at a commercial gym and think it's time to get serious, and need guidance

You won't be held back if you're advanced. You won't be pushed beyond your limits if you are a complete novice.

I have injuries and niggles. Is that a problem?

Absolutely fine, we adjust the workout. We all do the same workout but all the classes are scalable and adjustable based on fitness level and experience. We also have an in-house physio.

What will I be doing?

Sixty minutes of fully coached strength and fitness. Dumbbells, kettlebells, barbells, functional kit and bodyweight are at the forefront. There will always be elements of conditioning to get that pulse racing, and essential flexibility and mobility to get you moving better and keep you injury free.

No random workouts. You won't just sweat here. You'll get better.

Why should I be strength training? I'm too old for this.

Just not true. There is a common myth that as we age we must slow down and do less. We are designed to be physically active way beyond what can be classed as youth. Use it or lose it!

Why not just join a cheaper gym and do my own thing?

You can absolutely do that. You should be proud of yourself you turned up. There is more to it than that though.

Do you drive? Did you just jump in a car one day and zoom off, or did you have driving lessons? Head down to First Space and let us show you how to drive your car. (We have analogies for days!)

Yeah, but what do you do better than the other gyms?

There are some fantastic gyms and coaches around. Anyone saying they have some sort of cheat code and do it better than anyone else is just not telling you the truth.

We are passionate about all things strength and conditioning and how it changes your life for the better. We believe you should be too. That's the cheat code.

Is there a contract?

There is no lengthy contract. One month rolling payment, paid at the start of the month. That's it. On cancellation there is a 4 week notice period.
